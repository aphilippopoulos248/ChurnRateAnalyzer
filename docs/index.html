Phase 2 · Model

Predicting who will churn

Three models scored with 5-fold cross-validation, so every subscriber is predicted by a model that never saw them. The chosen model is logistic regression with interaction flags: as accurate as gradient boosting, with probabilities that match reality and drivers you can read.

Predicted vs actual churn

Points near the dashed line mean a predicted 56% really is about 56%. That matters because Phase 3 turns these probabilities into revenue at risk.

Engineered features

Logistic regression adds feature effects together, so it can't learn "A and B together" on its own. These flags hand it the combinations found in Phase 1.

Risk segments

A shallow decision tree grouped subscribers by their predicted risk
Phase 3 · Recommend

Retention plan

Gemini read the segment report and the charts and returned a structured plan. Segment figures come from the model; strategies and savings are Gemini's assumptions until tested.

Data is a synthetic subscriber dataset. Drivers are associations from historical data, not proven causes.
